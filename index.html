Muri iki gihe iterambere ry'ikoranabuhanga rifite uruhare rukomeye mu koroshya itumanaho no kugeza amakuru ku baturage. Ariko hari igihe abaturage,cyane cyane urubyiruko,
batamenya amakuru ajyanye n'ibibera mu murenge wabo, amatangazo mashya, ibikorwa by'iterambere ndetse n'aho bashobora kwitabaza igihe bafite ikibazo.
Uyu mushinga ugamije gukora urubuga rwa interineti rwihariye rw'umurenge, ruzajya rufasha abaturage kubona amakuru y'ingenzi mu buryo bworoshye.
Kuri uru rubuga hazajya hatangirwa amatangazo, hagashyirwa amafoto y'ibikorwa biherutse gukorwa, hagatangirwa amakuru ku bayobozi b'umurenge n'inshingano zabo,
ndetse n'uburyo abaturage bashobora kubageraho igihe bakeneye ubufasha. Ibi bizafasha guteza imbere imikoranire hagati y'abaturage n'ubuyobozi, gukorera mu mucyo no kongera uruhare rw'abaturage mu iterambere ry'umurenge wabo.
